# Merchant Settlements

Merchants are settled on a T+1 basis. Money from transactions captured today is sent to the merchant's bank account on the next business day.

Settlements are not processed on weekends or public holidays. Transactions from Friday, Saturday and Sunday are settled together on Monday.

A settlement is held automatically if the merchant's KYC documents have expired. The merchant must upload valid documents before the held money is released.

The platform fee is deducted before settlement. The settlement report shows the gross amount, fees, refunds and the final amount sent.
