# Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 14 days of a successful payment. After 14 days, refund requests are rejected automatically.

Refunds are always sent back to the original payment method. We never refund to a different card or wallet.

Card refunds take 5 to 7 business days to appear on the customer's statement. Wallet refunds (JazzCash, Easypaisa) are usually instant, but can take up to 24 hours.

Partial refunds are allowed. The total of all partial refunds on a transaction can never be more than the original amount.

A refund cannot be issued while the transaction is under an active chargeback dispute. The dispute must be resolved first.
